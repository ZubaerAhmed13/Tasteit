Source copyright policy: https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Wikibooks:Copyrights

Each record includes the exact revision, contributor/history link, licence URL and change notice. Links/formatting are converted to plain text; original primary ingredient quantities are retained and secondary parenthetical unit conversions are omitted where present. Procedure wording, recipe notes and introductory recipe context are retained. Reuse/adaptations of this recipe text must retain attribution and the same licence. This licence concerns these recipe texts; application code and separately credited photographs have their own terms. No dish photographs or publisher star ratings are invented for this collection. Category tags use source evidence with an explicit categoryEvidenceAt date; they are not cultural/dietary certification. Community instructions are source guidance, not tested cooking or food-safety certification.

The original 1,108 publisher records are unchanged. Their link-only methods are not replaced with different recipes. The additional 50 recipes have distinct identities.
